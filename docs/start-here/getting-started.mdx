Seeker Agent Connect (SAC) is an Android app for the Solana Seeker. It gives the agents, services and communities you already use one familiar place on your phone to reach you: a single inbox where you review each request or signal, decide, and see the result. When something needs a signature, **Seed Vault Wallet**, the wallet you already have, signs it.

You keep your existing agents and communities. Developers reach that interface without building a separate mobile app for the workflow: a server sends a request, or a publisher posts a signal, and SAC handles the review on the phone.

SAC never creates a wallet, never holds your wallet's keys, and never signs on its own. Connecting a server or adding a feed only lets that source send you something to review.

The SAC team operates the gateway. You never deploy or administer it; you integrate with it.

## What a request can be

Acknowledge a message, sign a message, transfer SOL or a token, swap, place a prediction order, or stake SKR. Every one waits for your tap on the phone. Acknowledgements finish in SAC; the others then open the wallet for your confirmation, except in a sandbox feed, where SAC stops before signing. See [Review requests and signals](/docs/reviewing-requests).
